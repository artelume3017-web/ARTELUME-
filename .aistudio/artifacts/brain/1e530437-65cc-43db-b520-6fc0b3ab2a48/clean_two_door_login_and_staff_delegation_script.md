### 2. Operational Workflows

#### A. Studio Director Access (Owner)
1. Open the studio link on any phone, tablet, or desktop.
2. Under the left card **"👑 Studio Director"**, tap **"⚡ Enter Studio as Director"**.
3. **Instant Entry**: You are logged in immediately with master authority over:
   - Branch 1 (Hyderabad) and Branch 2 (Vizag) via the top bar and sidebar switcher.
   - P&L gross margins, bank passbook ledgers, cash entries, and supplier purchase bills.
   - Staff creation, role assignment, and 4-digit PIN generation.

#### B. Branch Staff Access
1. Under the right card **"🏢 Branch Staff Portal"**, tap your branch:
   - **Branch 1: Hyderabad** (`AL-HYD`) OR **Branch 2: Vizag** (`AL-VZG`).
2. The staff list for that branch appears automatically.
3. Tap your name (e.g., `Vikram Sharma` or `Sneha Rao`).
4. Enter your **4-digit PIN** (e.g., `1234` or `5678`) and tap **"Enter App →"**.
5. **Instant Locked Entry**:
   - The app opens immediately locked to your branch.
   - All quotations and delivery challans automatically receive your branch code and bank details.
   - Financial ledgers and profit margins remain strictly protected from non-owner staff.
